# Wine Body

## Light-Bodied Wines
*Refreshing, crisp, and lower in alcohol, sitting gently on the palate like water or skimmed milk.*
- **Delicate**: Elegant and subtle; doesn't overwhelm the senses.
- **Crisp**: Bright and clean, typically driven by high, refreshing acidity.
- **Lean**: Lacking heavy fruit or oak weight; focused, tight, and precise.
- **Ethereal**: Incredibly light, airy, and nuanced.
- **Zesty**: Energetic and lively, often with a citrusy kick.
- **Racy**: High-acid, vibrant, and fast-moving across the palate.
- **Bright**: Fresh and illuminating, emphasizing primary fruit and acidity over weight.
- **Quaffable**: Easy-drinking, refreshing, and perfect for casual sipping.
- **Airy**: Lacks any sense of density; feels completely weightless.
- **Chiseled**: Sharp, clean, and structurally defined without any extra flab or fat.

## Medium-Bodied Wines
*The "chameleons" of wine. They offer a perfect balance—striking the middle ground.*
- **Balanced**: Harmonious, where the alcohol, fruit, and weight feel perfectly proportional.
- **Versatile**: Food-friendly; neither too heavy for fish nor too light for meat.
- **Supple**: Smooth and yielding on the palate, with no harsh or aggressive edges.
- **Round**: Lacks sharp edges; possesses a pleasing, gentle curve and softness in the mouth feel.
- **Fleshy**: Has a bit of fruit meatiness and substance, but stops short of being heavy.
- **Approachable**: Easy to enjoy, smooth, and well-suited to a variety of palates.
- **Juicy**: Fruit-forward and mouth-watering, with a pleasant, moderate weight.
- **Smooth**: Silky texture that glides across the mid-palate effortlessly.
- **Moderate**: Present and noticeable in weight, but completely unassuming.
- **Fluid**: Graceful movement across the tongue without being watery or viscous.

## Full-Bodied Wines
*Big, bold, and powerful wines. They coat the mouth with a rich, tongue-clinging viscosity.*
- **Rich**: Deep, flavor-packed, and physically weighty on the palate.
- **Opulent**: Luxurious, bold, and silky smooth; feels genuinely decadent.
- **Robust**: Full of strength, structure, and powerful alcohol or tannin.
- **Muscular**: Powerfully structured and intense; has a dominant presence.
- **Viscous**: Thick and heavy-textured; coats the sides of your mouth.
- **Mouth-filling**: Takes up physical space in the mouth; expansive and broad.
- **Dense**: Highly concentrated in both structural components and flavor profile.
- **Velvety**: Incredibly soft but heavy, like plush fabric sliding over the tongue.
- **Chewy**: Heavily textured with thick tannins, making it feel like you could almost bite into it.
- **Fat**: Highly viscous with broad texture and low apparent acidity.
