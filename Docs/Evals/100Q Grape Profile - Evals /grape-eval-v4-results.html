Grape profile eval v4: 100 varied questions, 3 runs

50 answerable, 25 false-claim and 25 unsupported questions across 67 grapes. The three runs used the same prompt and app build, and 99 of 100 answers were identical, so the runs repeat rather than add evidence.

Where the model stands

The axis starts at 70%. Blue is what the user sees; orange is only the answers Gemma wrote, which leaves out the app's own refusals and winery replies.

Results by question type

Every comparison, body, acidity, aroma and origin question was fully correct. Of the 15 full descriptions, 9 are fully correct and 4 are Kotlin's (reply-check refusals and the winery reply). Most ageing and summary failures are also Kotlin's.

What cost points, and who owns them

Gemma is graded only on facts Kotlin supplied, and every required fact was in the supplied context. Kotlin's failures are scored to Kotlin: all 9 zeros. Gemma owns 5 answers in run 1 (4 in runs 2 and 3), and none is a zero. Ageing and origin may be dropped from a description because of the six-sentence limit, but not when the question asks about that field.

Grades follow the 2/1/0 marker: 2 = covers what the question asked for, with nothing wrong, missed or invented; 1 = partly right, with something wrong, invented or missed; 0 = most or all of the answer is wrong, invented or missed. Gemma does not lose a point for leaving out facts Kotlin did not supply. For a full description, the five core fields (summary, body, acidity, tannin, aromas) earn a 2; ageing and origin may be dropped within the six-sentence limit. If a question asks about a field and Kotlin supplied it, leaving it out counts against Gemma. The run log's route check reports 97% because it misses the 6 reply-check refusals; it is not the decision accuracy. Claim metrics count sentences and required fields over the 75 answerable questions. Run 1 differs from runs 2 and 3 only in ASK4-001.

Caveat: these grades are Claude's reading of each answer against its context. They have not had the blind human check that v3 had.
