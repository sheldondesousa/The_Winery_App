# Wine Acidity

## 1. The Energy Framework

### Low Acidity
- **Synonyms**: Flabby, Soft, Flat, Plush, Languid.
- **Meaning**: The wine moves slowly, feels heavy, and lacks a refreshing kick.

### Medium Acidity
- **Synonyms**: Lively, Bright, Balanced, Fresh, Animated.
- **Meaning**: There is a pleasant, steady hum of refreshment that perfectly matches the fruit weight.

### High Acidity
- **Synonyms**: Electric, Racy, Vibrant, Zippy, Laser-focused.
- **Meaning**: The acidity jolts your senses immediately and races down the center of your palate with high energy.

## 2. The Texture & Edge Framework

### Low Acidity
- **Synonyms**: Round, Smooth, Fleshy, Broad, Coating.
- **Meaning**: The lack of acid allows the wine to spread out and coat your tongue smoothly without any sharp edges.

### Medium Acidity
- **Synonyms**: Defined, Clean, Crisp, Tailored, Snappy.
- **Meaning**: The wine has a clear shape and boundaries, keeping it neat and tidy on the palate.

### High Acidity
- **Synonyms**: Sharp, Chiseled, Linear, Angular, Cutting.
- **Meaning**: The acidity acts like a knife, cutting right through any sweetness or fat, leaving a tight, narrow sensation.

## 3. The Culinary & Flavor Framework

### Low Acidity
- **Synonyms**: Baked, Jammy, Mellow, Overripe, Low-toned.
- **Meaning**: Tastes like fruit that has been cooked down or left in the sun, where the natural tartness has faded away.

### Medium Acidity
- **Synonyms**: Juicy, Mouth-watering, Tangy, Fruity-tart, Zesty.
- **Meaning**: Mimics the refreshing splash of biting into a crisp green apple or a perfectly ripe orange.

### High Acidity
- **Synonyms**: Citric, Searing, Bracing, Tart, Pucker-inducing.
- **Meaning**: Feels exactly like sucking on a wedge of fresh lime, lemon, or a sour green plum.
