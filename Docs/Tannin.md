# Wine Tannin

## Smooth Tannins
*Integrated, gentle, and melted into the wine, offering a seamless texture.*
- **Silky**: Incredibly fine-grained and luxurious; glides effortlessly over the tongue.
- **Velvety**: Plush, soft, and thick, offering a gentle, fabric-like texture.
- **Supple**: Yielding, soft, and compliant, with absolutely no sharp or hard edges.
- **Resolved**: Older or well-made tannins that have softened and completely integrated over time.
- **Melted**: Tannins that have lost their aggressive grip and feel completely liquid and smooth.
- **Polished**: Buffed and refined, showing a highly sophisticated, smooth finish.
- **Seamless**: Continuous and smooth from the first sip to the swallow, with no textural bumps.
- **Satin**: Slick and lustrous, with a light, elegant, and low-friction glide.
- **Integrated**: Perfectly blended with the fruit and acidity, making them barely noticeable.
- **Cashmere**: Richly textured yet extraordinarily soft, conveying high-end comfort.

## Moderate Tannins
*Noticeable, pleasant structural framework without overpowering the fruit flavors.*
- **Structured**: Possesses a clear, firm architectural frame that gives the wine aging potential.
- **Chalky**: Feels like a fine, powdery coating on the gums.
- **Fine-grained**: Composed of tiny, microscopic structural elements rather than large, coarse blocks.
- **Firm**: Secure and present, holding the wine's shape tightly without being aggressive.
- **Grippy**: Noticeably grabs the inside of your cheeks with a pleasant, energetic tug.
- **Dusty**: Evokes the dry sensation of cocoa powder or fine vineyard dust on the finish.
- **Chewy**: Substantial and textured, giving the impression that the wine has physical mass to bite into.
- **Fleshy**: Well-padded tannins that are surrounded and softened by plenty of ripe fruit.
- **Textured**: Adds an interesting, tactile dimension and weight to the mid-palate.
- **Savory**: Earthy and rustic rather than sweet, contributing to a dry, food-friendly finish.

## Astringent Tannins
*Powerful, youthfully aggressive, and intensely drying.*
- **Aggressive**: Boldly forces its way to the front of the palate.
- **Drying**: Rapidly dehydrates the mouth, leaving your tongue feeling parched.
- **Harsh**: Coarse, rough, and unrefined; feels abrasive against the cheeks.
- **Auster**: Severely dry, strict, and uncompromising.
- **Green**: Stemmy, bitter, and underripe.
- **Angular**: Sharp and pointy, lacking any roundness or softening fruit weight.
- **Coarse**: Large-grained and sandpaper-like, creating a rough friction in the mouth.
- **Gripping**: Clings fiercely to the gums and refuses to let go.
- **Youthful**: Packed with tightly wound, unevolved energy.
- **Puckering**: Causes an involuntary contraction of the lips and cheeks.
