# Wine Sweetness

## Map 1: The Sommelier Synonyms

### Dry
- **Bone-dry**: Absolutely zero sugar; crisp, searingly clean, and refreshing.
- **Austere**: Stripped of sweetness; focused entirely on minerality and structure.
- **Savory**: Herbaceous, earthy, or salty, emphasizing non-fruit flavors.
- **Chiseled**: Sharp, precise, and completely free of any soft, sugary weight.

### Off-Dry / Medium
- **Off-dry**: Just a whisper of sugar; balances spice or intense acidity.
- **Lush**: Soft and round, with a gentle, comforting touch of sweetness.
- **Succulent**: Mouth-watering and juicy; tastes like perfectly ripe, dripping fruit.
- **Mellow**: Softened sugar levels that take the edge off a high-acid wine.

### Sweet
- **Lucious**: Thick, rich, and incredibly satisfying without being cloying.
- **Honeyed**: Displays a dense, nectar-like sweetness with complex floral notes.
- **Nectarous**: Feels like pure fruit syrup or essence coating the palate.
- **Syrupy**: Heavily concentrated sugar with high viscosity and a long, coating finish.

## Map 2: The Technical Scale

| Sweetness Tier | Residual Sugar (RS) | Classic European Terms | Global Wine Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Dry | 0 – 9 g/L | Brut (Sparkling), Trocken (German) | Sauvignon Blanc, Cabernet Sauvignon, Chianti |
| Off-Dry / Medium | 10 – 45 g/L | Extra Dry / Demi-Sec, Feinherb | Vouvray, Halbtrocken Riesling, Prosecco |
| Sweet | 45+ g/L | Doux, Süß / Sweet | Sauternes, Port, Icewine, Tokaji Aszú |

## Map 3: The Functional Scale

- **Dry (The Refresher)**: Cuts through rich, fatty dishes (like oysters, fried chicken, or cream sauces) by using high acidity and zero sugar to cleanly scrub the palate.
- **Off-Dry (The Fire Extinguisher)**: Acts as a shield against fiery heat and spice (like Thai, Indian, or Mexican cuisine). The touch of sugar coats the tongue and calms the burn of capsaicin.
- **Sweet (The Companion or Contrast)**: Pairs with desserts (the wine must always be sweeter than the food, or it will taste bitter). Alternatively, it contrasts beautifully with salty, pungent blue cheeses.
